Symptom      1
PROBLEM WITH I/O AND USB PORT AND OVER HAUL

   Item#                                   Serial#            Qty            Result
   Inspection Detail
MD-F3100                           2223100901701A2L                            1       Inspection
Operated time: 27442 hours (Shipped out in March, 2013)
<Appearance check>
As for the returned unit, with a visual inspection, it was confirmed that the whole exterior was
scratched and adhered with dirt, and that the head front cover was cracked.
<Operation check>
With an operation check, it was confirmed that the USB communication was not possible, and that
there was abnormality in the input/output operation of the terminal block.
<Internal check>
The unit was disassembled for inspection, and it was confirmed that the USB communication was not
possible due to the malfunction of the driver board.
Also, it was confirmed that abnormality of the input/output operation of the terminal block
occurred due to the malfunction of the terminal block board.
Moreover, it was confirmed that the above symptom was cleared due to the replacement of the
driver board/terminal block board.
Other than that, it was confirmed that oily stain intruded inside the unit, that the controller
upper/lower front covers were damaged, and that dirt adhered to all controller fans and air
filters.
<Occurrence factor>
As for the malfunction of the driver board/terminal block board, it is suspected to be due to the
effect of the electrical stress (noise/surge), and the deterioration of the components.
<Conclusion>
As for the returned unit, the replacement components for the driver board were not available.
Therefore, it is suggested that the driver board from Keyence test unit is reused to proceed with
the repair.
Please consider the repair necessity by replacing the reused driver board and the (A) parts,
cleaning, and readjusting.
Also, replacement of the (B) parts is recommended since deterioration might have been in progress
due to long-term usage.
Moreover, there will be no charge for the components since the reused driver board is a reused
product of Keyence test unit, however, please understand that the unit will not be covered by
repair warranty.

Symptom      1
      POWER LED GREEN LIGHT TOGGLE , ALSO WHEN CONNECT MC-P1 , IT SHOW NO DATA SIGNAL

                        Item#                                   Serial#            Qty            Result
                                                      Inspection Detail
          MD-V9900                           1969900906761A8B                            1       Inspection
          Operated time: 28279 hours (Shipped out in February, 2008)
          <Appearance check>
          As for the returned unit, with a visual inspection, it was observed that the head fan harness was
          jammed.
          <Operation check>
          With an operation check, the occurrence of the described symptom was confirmed.
          <Internal check>
          The unit was disassembled for inspection, and it was confirmed that the described symptom had
          occurred due to the failure on the 15V power supply inside the controller.
          With an operation check after replacing the 15V power supply, it was confirmed that marking laser
          output was declined to 9.7W (13.0W as of shipping) at laser power 100% setting by using an
          external power meter, due to misalignment of the oscillator optical system and dirt adhesion on
          the optical components inside the head front.
          Moreover, it was confirmed that both upper and lower front covers of the controller were damaged,
          and head fans / filters were adhered with dirt.
          <Occurrence factor>
          The suspected causes of the damaged 15V power supply are an effect of the adhesion of oil since
          the oil-stain adhered to the board, and/or applying electrical stress.
          The suspected cause of the misalignment of the oscillator optical system is applying of external
          stress (vibration, impact or thermal changes) or changes of condition over time.
          <Conclusion>
          As for the returned unit, please consider the repair necessities by replacing (A) parts,
          readjusting and cleaning.
          The (B) parts are recommended to be replaced since deterioration could have been in progress due
          to long term process.
          Regarding the dirt adhesion on the optical system components inside the head front, since
          significantly minute dust intruded, the packing (gasket) alone is suspected to be insufficient to
          fully prevent dust from intruding.
          As a countermeasure for this symptom, a connection port is installed on the rear side of the head
          to be able to perform air purging.
          Please consider performing air purging (supplying clean air for 24 hours) at the place of use.
          [Results on MD-V9900 series inspections upon arrival]

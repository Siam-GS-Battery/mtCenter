## หน้า 2

<Appearance check>
As for the returned unit, with a visual inspection, no abnormality was observed.

<Operation check>
With an operation check, the occurrence of laser power declined was confirmed.

<Internal check>
operation check was performed, and it was confirmed that marking laser output was declined to 2.8W (13.0W
as of shipping) at laser power 100% setting by using an external power meter due to the LD output was
declined
Other than that was found noise from all power supply.

<Occurrence factor>
It is considered that the factor of LD output decline could be the deterioration due to operated time, or
application of electrical stress (noise surge).

<Conclusion>
As for the returned unit, please consider the repair necessities by replacing LD, all power supply, and filter,
readjusting, and cleaning.

***this case claim LD as free of charge due to short term from last repair.

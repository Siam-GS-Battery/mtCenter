3. Brief Repair Progress Report:
<Appearance check>
With a visual inspection, no particular fault was found.

<Operation check>
With an operation check, the customer symptom was replicated system error 12 was show after turn power
on.

< Internal check >
The unit was disassembled for inspection, the error occurred from the malfunction of LD power supply. The
symptom was improved after replaced LD power supply.
below are speculated factors of the damage.
-electrical stress such as surge noise on the power supply line.
-corrosion from effects of condensation from moisture and/or of a corrosive gas.
-damage due to heat on usage under high temperature environment exceeding the specification.
other than that I/O terminal was communication error.

<Conclusion>
In order to overcome the symptom, the LD power supply,filter and terminal board needed to be replaced.
The adjustment process and cleaning process had been performed as well.

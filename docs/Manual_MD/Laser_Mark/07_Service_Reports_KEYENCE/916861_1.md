Symptom      1
      THE UNIT WAS LD HIGH TEMPERATURE AND     POWER DECLINED

                        Item#                                   Serial#            Qty            Result
                                                      Inspection Detail
          MD-V9900W                          196990W916861A9E                            1       Inspection
          Operated time: 7007 hours (Shipped out in May, 2009)
          <Appearance check>
          As for the returned unit, with a visual inspection, no abnormality was observed.
          <Operation check>
          With an operation check, it was confirmed that [E100: LD High Temperature Error] occurred.
          <Internal check>
          The unit was disassembled for inspection, and it was confirmed that the described symptom
          occurred due to damage of two 12V power supply (for Peltier) inside controller.
          Since 12V power supply of last repair one is power supply for console, it is different from the
          power supply which was damaged this time.
          12V power supply was replaced, and operation check was performed, and marking laser output was
          found declined to 9.3W (13.0W as of shipping) at laser power 100% setting by using the external
          power meter. This is caused by the dirt adhesion on the optical system components inside of the
          head front and misalign of oscillator optical system.
          Other than that, it was confirmed that non-Keyence air filter was installed.
          <Occurrence factor>
          Based on the disconnection of fuse, the suspected causes of the damage for the 12V power supply
          are applying of voltage which greatly exceeds the specifications to the power supply circuit due
          to surge/noise from the power supply line, and deterioration due to long-term usage.
          The suspected cause of the misalignment of oscillator optical system is applying external stress
          (vibration, impact or thermal changes).
          Besides the vibration and shocks from usage environment, please check if there were any impacts
          during installation on the device, or if there are any transportation with other than the regular
          packing or with the state of being incorporated in the device.
          <Conclusion>
          As for the returned unit, please consider the repair necessities by replacing (A) parts,
          readjusting and cleaning.
          Also, replacement of 24V power supply, 15V power supply and LD power supply is recommended since
          these can be damaged as well as damaged power supply.
          Other (B) parts are recommended to be replaced since deterioration could have been in progress
          due to long term process.
          Please kindly consider reviewing the usage environment once again since the power supply had been
          damaged in the past.

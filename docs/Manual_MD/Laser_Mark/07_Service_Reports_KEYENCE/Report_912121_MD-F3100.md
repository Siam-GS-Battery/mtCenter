                                                       Inspection Detail
          MD-F3100                           2223100912121A3L                            1       Inspection
          Operated time: 53210 hours (Shipped out in February, 2014)
          <Appearance check>
          With a visual inspection, it was confirmed that the head front cover was cracked.
          <Operation check>
          With an operation check, [E130: System Error 21] was not replicated, however, the multiple
          occurrences were confirmed on the error history.
          Also, it was confirmed that marking laser output was declined to 28.7W (30.0W as of shipping) at
          laser power 100% setting by using an external power meter, and that the I/O operation of the
          terminal block had abnormality.
          <Internal check>
          The unit was disassembled for inspection, and it was confirmed that marking laser output was
          declined due to dirt on the head front optical system, and that the output was recovered by
          cleaning.
          Also, it was confirmed that the malfunction of the terminal block I/O was generated due to the
          damage on the terminal block board.
          Other than that, it was confirmed that the controller upper/lower front covers were damaged, and
          that the controller fan and air filter were adhered with dirt.
          <Occurrence factor>
          The suspected factor of the damage on the terminal block board is the effect of application of
          electrical stress (noise, surge), adhesion of dirt, or deterioration of components.
          As for [E130: System Error 21], this is the error which is generated when the communication
          between the laser unit and the controller had abnormality.
          The factor could not be identified since the symptom was not replicated; however, it is suspected
          that it could have been generated due to the effect of application of electrical stress (noise,
          surge) and malfunction of the fiber unit control power supply and the LD power supply.
          <Conclusion>
          As for the repair of this unit, please consider the repair necessity by replacing the (A) parts,
          readjusting and cleaning.
          Replacement of the fiber unit control power supply and the LD power supply is recommended since
          these could have been occurrence factors of [E130: System Error 21].
          In addition, replacement of the other (B) parts is recommended since the deterioration may be in
          progress due to passage of long term.

## หน้า 2

Introduction
                             The laser marker "MD-F3000 Series" uses a Fiber laser to provide high quality
                             marking on a 2D plane (X, Y), and it also provides controls in the (Z) direction to
                             produce marking on a 3D plane.
                             This User’s Manual provides general information regarding installation in order
                             to ensure safe and accurate performance, including I/O connections to external
                             devices and product maintenance.
                             To configure or operate the laser marker, use the touch panel console (MC-P1)
                             or the laser marker setting software, MARKING BUILDER 2 (sold separately).
                             For more about these operations, see the PDF manual stored in the CD-ROM.
                             Always keep this manual in a safe place for future reference.

• KEYENCE can not guarantee the function or performance of the MD-F3000 series if it is used in a manner that

Laser Specifications
The MD-F3000 Series uses a Fiber laser. Based on the laser safety requirements from IEC60825-1 and FDA(CDRH)21CFR
Part 1040.10, this product is classified as Class 4 (marking laser) and Class 2 (guide laser) laser product.
International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC) classify the safety of a product according to the output level of the laser
product, for the purpose of preventing laser beam injury to workers who handle the laser product or who may be
exposed to the laser beam. The following is the classification and specifications of the laser.

Class 4 (marking laser)
Class 4 lasers are defined as "Laser products for which intrabeam viewing and skin exposure is hazardous and for which
the viewing of diffuse reflections may be hazardous. These lasers also often represent a fire hazard."

## หน้า 9

Safety Measures
The MD-F3000 series is a Class 4 laser product. Take all appropriate safety measures.

 Use of remote interlock connector
   Connect the remote interlock connector (Emergency stop input terminal) to an emergency master disconnect
   interlock or to room, door, or fixture interlocks.

 Key control
   To prevent operation of the laser system by unauthorized users, the key should be managed by the Laser Safety Officer.

 Setting the warning indication sign and controlled area
   Display a warning sign at the entrance to the area in which the MD-F3000 series is installed in order to ensure that
   workers and outsiders are informed about the dangers.

 Terminating the beam path
   Terminate the path of the laser beam emitted from the laser product with a diffuse reflector or absorber with
   appropriate reflectance and heat absorption characteristics. Never use a specular type reflector.

 Eye protection
   In the controlled area in which the MD-F3000 series is installed, make it a habit to wear protective eye wear in case of
   accidental exposure to laser emission. In addition, always wear the protective eye wear when performing maintenance.

 Appointing a laser safety officer
   Appoint a safety officer who has knowledge and experience in handling laser products in order to enforce
   safety management. The responsibilities of the Laser Safety Officer are as follows:
        1. Suggesting prevention measures related to laser emission
        2. Setting up the laser controlled area (area in which there is a risk of exposure to laser emission from the laser products)
        3. Managing the key for the key-operated power switch
        4. Checking the protective equipment and its use
        5. Training operators

## หน้า 12

4.   Do not use the MD-F3000 Series in an environment with large amounts of dust or an oily mist.


     5.   If the air filters become dirty, clean or replace them.
          Failure to clean the filter may cause clogs, reducing the efficiency of the air cooling. This can reduce
          the laser output and cause damage to the marking unit.
          Perform regular maintenance on the filter.


     6.   Prevent dust or dirt from becoming affixed to the
          marking unit lens.
          Dust or dirt adhered to the lens may cause
          problems such as burning of the lens surface,
          pealing of the coating, and misprinted marking.
          Regularly clean dirt from the lens by using a
          blower to blast clean air across the surface or by
          cleaning with acetone or ethanol.


     7.   Use the MD-F3000 controller unit and the MD-F3000 marking unit together.
          Connecting the marking unit or controller unit of another model leads to product damage.
          Follow the steps below when connecting the MD-F3000 Series to prevent errors due to noise.
          • Check that the controller input and output connections are performed correctly.
          • Isolate the connection cables and external control cables from other power lines. Do not bind
            them together.
          • Use an AC power line just for the controller. Do not use an AC power line that is used by other
            devices.
          • Be sure to establish Class D grounding for power grounding conductors.


     8.   The MD-F3000 Series is a precision marking device. Be sure to use the packing material specified
          by KEYENCE for transportation.
          Otherwise, transportation could cause malfunctions or damage. Keep the packaging materials for
          future use.

Precautions for Low-voltage Directive and Machinery Directive

     Installation
     You must perform an appropriate installation of the MD-F3000 Series after conducting a sufficient risk assessment for the
     target machine.
     The MD-F3000 Series is designed as Class I Equipment. Therefore, be sure to connect the protective conductor terminal
     on the power terminal block to the protective earthing conductor in building installation.
     A disconnecting device must be provided near the MD-F3000 Series.
     (Recommended breaking capacity: 15A)
     The disconnecting device must be one of the following types:
     • A switch, with or without fuses, in accordance with IEC 60947-3, utilization category AC-23B or DC-23B;
     • A circuit-breaker suitable for isolation in accordance with IEC 60947-2.
     Do not use the MD-F3000 series at 1000 m altitude or more.

     Replacing a fuse
     The fuse can be replaced in the MD-F3000 Series. When replacing the fuse, use a fuse that meets the following rating
     and complies with the European Product Safety Standard.

Precautions on CSA certification

Installation

• The MD-F3000 Series is designed as a Class I Equipment. Be sure to connect the protective conductor terminal
  on the power terminal block to the protective earthing conductor in building installation.
  Also, be sure to install a switch to disconnect from supply source or a circuit-breaker near the MD-F3000 Series

1-1 3D Laser Features and Elements
                                     This chapter describes features and elements of the 3D marking function of the
                                     MD-F3000 Series.


  1          Features
Overview
             Conventional laser markers only control the laser in the X and Y directions. The MD-F3000 Series, however,
             includes a 3-axis simultaneous control system that also controls the laser in the Z direction. With this new system,
             the laser spot is fully controlled to match the shape of the marking object and the variations in distance.

             • This system helps to suppress changes in the spot size, variations in the marking area, and warping around the
               minimum limits at the center of the marking area and around the marking area.
             • The laser marker can handle a variety of shapes, including uneven surfaces, tilted surfaces, cylinders, or cones,
               while controlling the laser spot in real time to produce perfect marking on 3D objects.


             3D Laser Control Elements
             The laser marker controls the focus distance of the laser beam emitted from the laser oscillator while varying the
             beam diameter with the Z scanner. The focus position is ±21mm in any direction within a 3D space from the
             standard distance. The focus is varied in real time.
             The laser beam controlled by the Z scanner scans the marking object with the X/Y scanner. The laser emissions
             are controlled to effectively mark text and figures on a 3D surface.

5-3 Cleaning and Replacing the Air Filter
                                                 Clean the air filter of the controller periodically.

               Remove the air filter from the controller to clean it. When the air filter is used over a long period of time, dirt may
               adhere to it resulting in deteriorated performance, and an error may occur. Replace or clean the air filter whenever
               dust or dirt has built up on the surface of the filter.


                                                                                            Caution
                   • If the MD-F3000 Series is used with a dirty air filter, the temperature inside the controller rises, weakening the laser power or
                      causing malfunction.
                   • Before cleaning the air filter of the controller, be sure to turn off the power.
                   • After removing the air filter, keep the ventilation openings free from any foreign matter. Foreign substances that enter the
                      ventilation port may cause a breakdown of the unit.

               * Air filters are provided as replacement parts (OP-84216 MD-V2 air filter). Contact your nearest KEYENCE office.

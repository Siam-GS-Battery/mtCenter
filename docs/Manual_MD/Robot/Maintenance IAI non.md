1. Actuator Maintenance – PM plan

    • External Visual inspection


     • Internal inspection


     1.   Check whether dust or other foreign matter has gotten inside
     2.   Check the lubrication state.
     The lubrication may have turned brown. This is not a problem as long as the moving surface still coating
     with shiny grease.
     If the grease is mixed with dust and does not have a shiny appearance, or if the grease has lost its efficacy
     due to prolonged use, then clean each section and reapply grease.

  Rod and Radial type Model relate : RCP2-RA, RCP3-RA, RCP6-RA, RCA-RA,
                                     RCS2-RA/SRA/RGS/RGD, RCS4-RA
  Ball Screw
            1.   Remove the thin head screw on actuator body.
            2.   Move the rod by at least one half the stroke.
            3.   Apply grease to the ball screw, spray grease into the screw hole within 1second.
            4.   After grease has been applied, move the rod back and forth to let the grease spread evenly.
            5.   Attach the thin head screw as remove from(1) return to original position.


                                                                                Spray


 Caution:
     1. Never use spray oil instead of grease. always use spray grease.
     2. keep the spraying time to within 1 second.
     3. Do not spray grease continuously for more than 1 second or repeat spraying twice or more.
     If an excess amount of grease is applied, oil may flow into the electronic components and cause malfunction

## หน้า 40

1. Actuator Maintenance – Motor replacement
   Servo motor, with 『Ａ』, 『I』 and 『WA』 encoder
   When replacing the motor, handle the replacement motor with due care.
   The actuator has been shipped with the encode adjusted to an optimal position.
   Don’t crush the encoder unit. It may displace the encoder, thus impairing proper actuator operation

    Procedures:
    1. Remove a broken motor.
    2. Move the slider to a position where Z-phase turns on. (slider distance from the mechanical end depend
        on actuator model).
    3. Mark the shaft and body of the replacement motor to prevent the motor shaft position from deviating.
    4. Attach the replacement motor into the actuator coupling or motor holder for reverse type.
    5. Perform homing using a PC or teaching pendant and check for deviation from the initial home position.

## หน้า 43

1. Actuator Maintenance – Motor replacement
    Straight type’s Motor replacement method

 [Procedure – Motor unit]
 1. Remove the fixing screw affixing the actuator and the motor unit with a 2mm hex wrench.
 2. Detach the motor unit.
 3. Make the profiles on the actuator side and motor unit side aligned so the projection matches to the slit
 4. Attach the motor unit for replacement with the projection being matched with the slit.
 5. Tighten the fixing screw to affixing the motor unit to the actuator with 2mm hex wrench.
 6. Perform homing using a PC or teaching pendant and check for deviation from the initial home position.

1. Actuator Maintenance – Motor replacement
    Straight type’s Motor replacement method


 [Procedure – only Motor]
 1. Remove the motor cover. The motor cover will come off.
 2. Disconnect the motor connector and encoder connector.
 3. Remove the screws as retaining the motor. And pull out the motor.
 4. (*)Align the slit on the actuator main body and the protrusion on the motor for replacement.
 5. Insert the motor after confirming that the direction of the protrusion on the main unit and the slit on the
      motor are in the same position.
 6. Tighten the screws to hold the motor.
 7. (*)Plug the motor connector and encoder connector.
 8. (**)Attach the motor cover.
 9. Hold the motor cover with screws and tighten them.
 10. Perform homing using a PC or teaching pendant and check for deviation from the initial home position.

    Reverse type’s Motor replacement method

 [Procedure ]
 1. Remove the pulley cover.
 2. Remove the screws that holding motor cover and remove the motor cover.
 3. Loosen the bolts for holding motor. And remove the reduction belt.
 4. Remove the bolts for holding motor and remove motor from actuator.
 5. Install the new motor and temporarily tighten the tension adjustment bolts (bolt for holding motor) to
      hang the timing belt.
 6. Attached motor cover and pulley cover
 7. Perform homing using a PC or teaching pendant and check for deviation from the initial home position.
